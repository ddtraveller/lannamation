Chang Ngu ตำนานช้างงูThe snake that became an elephant: a Lanna Tales cartoon, told in Kam Mueang. Plan of 23 October 2026.

The Black Khom take Yonok and send its young king to be a village headman, paying gold every year. A novice they turned away is born again as the headman's son, Phrom. At thirteen a deva sends him to the Mae Khong, where three giant snakes float down. He grabs the third by the neck, with fifty village children behind him, and it becomes a white elephant. It will not leave the river until his big brother brings a plate beaten from a thousand of gold and strikes it. At sixteen Phrom rides it against the Khom, who run at the sight of it. Indra spares them with a wall of stone, the elephant goes back into its hill as a snake, and Phrom gives the place of honour to his brother.

25 scenes, the tale 16:35 long with 122 narration lines; then about 150 s of credits. With the hero film (97.75 s) and the studio card (12 s) first, the whole film is 20:54.

The rule: the chronicle first

Owner, 23 October 2026: "Go with historical accuracy as much as possible." Where the sources differ, this plan follows the Singhanawat chronicle (ตำนานสิงหนวติกุมาร), read in its 1936 typed text on Thai Wikisource and checked against the 1973 edition's scans. The folk tellings (the Lanna Folktales cartoon, the Chiang Saen school's page, the Ministry of Culture's page on the statue) fill in only where the chronicle is silent. Two departures from it, both for children and both the owner's: the novice's death is not shown, and the queen's craving for Khom blood is left out.

How it gets made

As docs/kit/films.md, "A new tale, from start to finish": this plan and its words (story.py, ../tale_plan.py); the narration spoken line by line and fitted to its scenes; the new pieces, candidates first; each scene on its own (sNN_*.js and sNN.html), checked with stills, kit_check.py, pop_check.py and arms_check.py; then the film page, its music and sounds, the render on two machines and the join with the hero film and the studio's card.
