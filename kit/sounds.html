The kit's sounds

Voices for the kit's creatures. Most are made by howl.py from an outline of numbers in sounds.py: how its pitch, its loudness and its mouth go over time; nothing in them was recorded. A few are recordings their makers gave to everyone, fetched by sounds.py; each says where it is from. Make and fetch them first, then open this page again:

python sounds.py  (every sound into out/sounds/; python checks/sound_check.py checks them)

No sounds made yet: run python sounds.py in the kit's folder.
