# The Maeng film's nature: true to old Lanna, and as beautiful as the hero's pond

The owner, 2026-10-27: "Look through all the lanna items in the kit including fruit, insects, tools, decorations,
etc. and really look through all the scenes with a fresh eye and try to make them beautiful", "use the pond scene
[of the hero film] for a template as to the level of beauty I'm asking for. Also, be true and real more than
anything", "make it really beautiful though whatever the case". Asked, the owner chose: the fields and the plants
follow the seasons of the story; what is not true goes, earlier approved touches too; "true" is true to the tale's
old Lanna, not to Chiang Rai today; and where a fact is not confirmed, "if it's 70%+ likely go with it".

The facts, each with its source, are in `nature_research.md` (researched the same day). The place: the lowland of
Mueang Chiang Rai round Doi Khao Khwai (Wat Phra That Doi Khao Khwai, Rop Wiang), a low hill of about 400-500 m.

## What is out, and why

- **From the Americas, so not in old Lanna:** frangipani (also a temple tree in the north, จำปา, not a yard's),
  bougainvillea, marigold, papaya, pineapple, bua tong (a Mexican weed, and mostly at 600-1,200 m), dragon fruit.
- **Of the high doi, not a 400-500 m hill:** nang phaya suea khrong (*Prunus cerasoides*, from about 1,000 m), goral,
  silver pheasant, miang tea gardens.
- **Not of this region:** durian, mangosteen, rambutan; the house crow and the rose-ringed parakeet; the snail-eating
  turtle.
- **Out of season** in the scene they would be in: fireflies outside May-October; golden shower (ratchaphruek) outside
  March-May; flame of the forest (thongkwao) and the red cotton tree (ngio) outside February-April; the wild orchid
  ueang phueng outside March-April; the Chinese pond heron outside the cold season.
- **Allowed, as old Lanna had them:** the great hornbill, green peafowl and gibbons, gone from round Chiang Rai today
  but there long ago, in the forest away from the village.
