ฉากมีชีวิต · Living Scenes · 生动的场景

A quiet picture of the north of Thailand that keeps changing: a forest, a village, a farm, a market, the world of the old stories, or the fish of a river pool, seen from under the water. Every bird, animal, fish and insect in it decides for itself what to do next. ภาพชนบทล้านนาที่มีชีวิต สัตว์ทุกตัวตัดสินใจเองว่าจะทำอะไรต่อ · 北泰的画面在变化，每只动物自己决定下一步做什么。

How they decide: each kind has its own ways, written from how the real animal behaves. A buffalo grazes most of the day and wallows at noon; an egret walks at its feet for the insects it stirs up; a hen scratches and pecks, and her chicks run back to her when they stray; a grasshopper sits still until something comes close. Each one weighs its ways by the hour, by how hungry and how tired it is, and by who is near, and picks one.

Watch, or press 🔈 for their sounds and the sounds around them, and tick 🎵 for quiet music made for each place. Nothing to do and nothing to win: it is a fish tank.
